Support high-speed (14x-100x real-time) audio-only MP3 capture on download-blocked SharePoint Meeting Recaps (g_fileInfo.isDownloadBlocked=true in streamembed.aspx) by triggering muted in-page playback ('Play video'), stripping the video/mp4 track, collecting and decrypting only the audio/mp4 (mp4a.40.2) fMP4 stream (part=initsegment + part=mediasegment with track=audio&quality=audcopy, segmentTime=k*wsd up to ppd via oneDrive.transcode) via WebCrypto AES-CBC (window.crypto.subtle.decrypt) parallel fetch/decrypt and MSE MediaSource/SourceBuffer hooks with 16x playbackRate and currentTime stepping, decoding via AudioContext.decodeAudioData to 44.1 kHz mono PCM, and encoding to MP3.
