Enable post-meeting Recap MP3 audio capture when opted in via CLI flag or configuration in `internal/capture/session.go` and `internal/cli/join.go` (Size: XS | Complexity: low, domain: code, posture: test-first, files: `internal/capture/session.go`, `internal/cli/join.go`).

Scope and approach:
1. In `internal/capture/session.go` (`Session.Run`), remove the `if rm, ok := m.(browser.RecapMeeting); ok && rm.IsRecap() { captionsOnly = true }` override so `captionsOnly := s.cfg.CaptionsOnly` (`!cfg.CaptureAudio`) governs both live meetings and Meeting Recaps: when `CaptionsOnly` is `true` (default), Recap skips audio and writes only the `.md` file; when `CaptionsOnly` is `false` (via `--audio`, `--recap-audio`, `capture_audio: true`, or `audio: true`), `Session.Run` creates both `.md` and `.mp3` files and runs `m.CaptureAudio(runCtx, out.Audio)` concurrently with `m.EnableCaptions(runCtx, sink)`.
2. In `internal/cli/join.go`, add `recapAudio bool` to `captureFlags`, bind `--recap-audio` (`"also capture Meeting Recap audio to an MP3 file"`), update `--audio` description to `"also capture meeting or recap audio to an MP3 file"`, apply `--recap-audio` to `cfg.CaptureAudio` in `applyCaptureFlags`, and forward `--recap-audio` / `--recap-audio=false` in `inheritedQueueFlags`.

Acceptance criteria:
1. `Session.Run` honors `SessionConfig.CaptionsOnly` for `RecapMeeting` implementations: skips audio and creates only `.md` when `CaptionsOnly` is `true`, and creates both `.md` and `.mp3` and invokes `CaptureAudio` when `CaptionsOnly` is `false`.
2. `--audio` and `--recap-audio` flags on `root`, `captions`, and `join` set `cfg.CaptureAudio` and are forwarded by `inheritedQueueFlags` for `--queue` runs.
3. `go test -race ./internal/cli/...` passes cleanly. Source stash: DF1F461F.
