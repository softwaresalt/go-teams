Make the direct-exec browser launcher (`launchCleanHeadedBrowser` / `cleanBrowserArgs` / `cleanHeadedBrowserArgs` in `internal/browser/recap.go`) honor `Options.DisableSandbox` and `TEAMS_DISABLE_SANDBOX` (`"1"` or `"true"`) while keeping the Chromium sandbox enabled by default, with unit tests in `internal/browser/recap_test.go` (Size: XS | Complexity: low, domain: code, posture: test-first, files: `internal/browser/recap.go`, `internal/browser/recap_test.go`).

Scope and approach:
1. In `internal/browser/recap.go` (applying cleanly on top of `015.001-T` after `015-S` and `016-S` merge), add `cleanBrowserArgsForOptions(port int, profileDir string, opts Options, initialURL string) []string` (and wire `cleanBrowserArgs`, `cleanHeadedBrowserArgs`, and `launchCleanHeadedBrowser` through it) so that:
   - When `shouldDisableSandbox(opts)` is `false` (default), `--no-sandbox` and `--disable-setuid-sandbox` are NOT emitted.
   - When `shouldDisableSandbox(opts)` is `true` (`opts.DisableSandbox == true` or `TEAMS_DISABLE_SANDBOX` is `"1"` or `"true"`), `"--no-sandbox"` and `"--disable-setuid-sandbox"` are appended before the trailing `initialURL` argument (while `AutomationControlled` remains absent).
2. In `internal/browser/recap_test.go`, set `t.Setenv("TEAMS_DISABLE_SANDBOX", "")` in the default `TestCleanHeadedBrowserFlags` and `TestCleanHeadlessBrowserFlags` assertions and add test cases verifying that both `Options{DisableSandbox: true}` and `TEAMS_DISABLE_SANDBOX=1` / `"true"` emit `--no-sandbox` and `--disable-setuid-sandbox` before `initialURL`.

Acceptance criteria:
1. By default (`DisableSandbox == false` and `TEAMS_DISABLE_SANDBOX` unset), direct-exec browser args do not include `--no-sandbox` or `--disable-setuid-sandbox`.
2. When `Options.DisableSandbox` is `true` or `TEAMS_DISABLE_SANDBOX` is `"1"` or `"true"`, direct-exec browser args include `--no-sandbox` and `--disable-setuid-sandbox` before `initialURL`.
3. `go test -race ./internal/browser/...` passes cleanly. Source stash: FC763C17 (PR #33, review-thread PRRT_kwDOTStvas62BKaF, 013.003-T / 013-F / 013-S).
