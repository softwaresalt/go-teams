# Scheduled and Protocol-Driven Live Call Capture R1-R15 (022-S)

## Problem Frame

<!-- BEGIN:problem-frame -->
Stash entry 77FC68CA (feature, medium) and brainstorm spec docs/product-specs/2026-11-13-scheduled-live-call-capture-requirements.md (R1-R15) require bounded and activity-aware live capture (--duration nominal window + overtime continuation while live caption mutations or PCM voice audio activity persists + automatic shutdown on call-end teardown or --idle-timeout silence), per-meeting MeetingSchedule overrides and fallback meeting title propagation, one-shot at: timestamps alongside cron:, configurable lobby_timeout, protocol-driven capture modes (ephemeral-live, live-with-summary, past-recap, auto), Idea 2C hybrid delayed post-call AI Summary capture (--ai-summary-delay in-process timer + crash-safe transcription_queue.txt --append-ai-summary --not-before fallback), batch --queue catch-up into configured output_dir, and operator documentation.
<!-- END:problem-frame -->

## Options

<!-- BEGIN:options -->
* Option A: Hard context timeout at --duration and holding Chromium open for 30m after live calls for AI Summary (rejected: cuts off overtime meetings, blocks the single Chromium profile during the 30m wait, and loses delayed summaries on process exit).
* Option B (chosen): Activity-extended nominal duration + overtime continuation + idle-timeout/call-end shutdown + Idea 2C hybrid timer and crash-safe queue fallback decomposed into 11 width-isolated tasks (022.001-T..022.011-T) under feature 022-F and shipment 022-S (chained after 021-S via blocks dependency).
<!-- END:options -->

## Chosen Direction

<!-- BEGIN:chosen-direction -->
Option B (11 width-isolated tasks 022.001-T..022.011-T under feature 022-F and shipment 022-S, sequenced after 021-S via backlogit dep add 022-S 021-S --type blocks): 022.001-T (internal/config R1/R2/R4/R7/R9/R12/R13/R14/R15 fields, aliases, and validation), 022.002-T (internal/scheduler cron + one-shot at triggers and validation R7/R8), 022.003-T (internal/browser callEndedJS, interim caption heartbeats, and PCM RMS voice activity detection R3/R11/R12), 022.004-T (internal/browser LobbyTimeout, activity callbacks, and live call-end polling R3/R9/R11/R12), 022.005-T (internal/browser unit tests for voice RMS, interim caption heartbeats, call-end probes, and lobby timeout), 022.006-T (internal/capture activity-aware nominal duration, overtime continuation, idle-timeout shutdown, fallback MeetingTitle, and AppendAISummaryToFile R1/R2/R3/R5/R11/R12/R13/R14), 022.007-T (internal/capture unit tests), 022.008-T (internal/cli root/join/capture flags, mode resolution, and Idea 2C hybrid delayed AI summary timer + queue fallback R1/R9/R12/R13/R14), 022.009-T (internal/cli queue --append-ai-summary/--not-before and schedule flag binding, per-meeting overrides, and profile busy-gate release during ai_summary_delay R2/R4-R8/R14/R15), 022.010-T (internal/cli unit and integration tests), and 022.011-T (README.md, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, and config.example.yaml documentation R10).
<!-- END:chosen-direction -->
