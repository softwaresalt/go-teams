Reconcile `.github/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md` (Step 0(c) linked-deliberation extension, Cascade Close Sub-Procedure preamble/step 3/step 5, post-cascade source-artifact cleanup, and Quality Criteria) and `.github/agents/_ship.agent.md` (Post-Merge Closure Step 7 "Source artifact cleanup", lines ~856–859) with backlogit 1.12 engine behavior and `autoharness.gates.shipment_closure.compute_linked_deliberation_disposition` (`LinkedDeliberationOutcome.RETAINED_DESCRIPTION_MENTION` and INV-12 outcome precedence) (Size: S | Complexity: low, domain: docs, posture: specification reconciliation, files: `.github/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md`, `.github/agents/_ship.agent.md`).

Scope and approach:
1. In Step 0(c) ("Linked-deliberation snapshot extension") of `.github/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md`, distinguish link kinds (`custom_fields.source_deliberation_id` vs. description/references matches) and record each validated linked deliberation's pre-close INV-12 disposition per `compute_linked_deliberation_disposition` (`retained_read_error -> retained_ambiguous -> already-archived -> retained_engine_unverified -> retained_live_status -> retained_shared_reference -> retained_description_mention -> archive`), noting that a deliberation linked only via description/references without `custom_fields.source_deliberation_id` resolves to `retained_description_mention` because backlogit 1.12's `ShipShipment` retains it.
2. In Cascade Close Sub-Procedure preamble, step 3, and step 5 of `.github/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md`:
   - Only deliberations classified as `expected_cascade` (`archive` / `PLANNED_ARCHIVE` under `compute_linked_deliberation_disposition` — linked via `custom_fields.source_deliberation_id` and not matching any `already-archived` or `retained_*` rule) belong in BOTH `allowed_ids` AND `required_ids` for the `backlogit_ship_shipment` cascade invocation itself (while `already-archived` deliberations remain excluded from `required_ids` and tolerated in `allowed_ids`).
   - Deliberations classified into any of the retained dispositions (`retained_description_mention`, `retained_shared_reference`, `retained_live_status`, as well as `retained_engine_unverified`, `retained_ambiguous`, `retained_read_error`) are EXCLUDED from `required_ids` AND EXCLUDED from `allowed_ids` for the `backlogit_ship_shipment` cascade call.
   - Add an explicit **pre/post retained-deliberation preservation check** in Step 3: if any retained deliberation (especially `retained_shared_reference` or `retained_live_status`, which must remain live in `.backlogit/queue/` for other unshipped work) appears in `archived_ids` or is missing from `.backlogit/queue/` immediately after the cascade call, the skill MUST halt with `unexpected-cascade` (`HALT — cascade archived retained deliberation {id}, revert required`) and run the `git-revert-on-cascade` recovery procedure.
   - Specify in post-cascade source-artifact cleanup (Step 4 / post-cascade cleanup) that ONLY a `retained_description_mention` deliberation with `status == "queued"` whose sole referencing feature just shipped and that has **zero remaining live references** in `.backlogit/queue/` (or an `archive` disposition deliberation not yet archived) may be archived explicitly by the agent (`backlogit archive <dl_id>`), whereas deliberations with disposition `retained_shared_reference`, `retained_live_status`, `retained_engine_unverified`, `retained_ambiguous`, or `retained_read_error` MUST remain in `.backlogit/queue/` untouched.
3. Update the Cascade Close Sub-Procedure bullet in `## Quality Criteria` of `.github/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md` to match Step 0(c) and step 3.
4. In `.github/agents/_ship.agent.md` Post-Merge Closure Step 7 ("Source artifact cleanup", lines ~856–859), replace the unconditional `backlogit_archive_item` call on every unarchived `custom_fields.source_deliberation_id` with disposition-aware cleanup that honors the exact same INV-12 retention dispositions and live-reference checks from `shipment-reconcile` Step 0(c) / `compute_linked_deliberation_disposition`:
   - Only archive a linked deliberation during post-merge source-artifact cleanup when its Step 0(c) / `compute_linked_deliberation_disposition` outcome is `archive` (if not already archived by the Step 1 cascade) OR `retained_description_mention` with `status == "queued"` and **zero remaining live references** in `.backlogit/queue/` after the shipment's features and tasks have been archived.
   - Never archive a deliberation whose disposition is `retained_shared_reference`, `retained_live_status`, `retained_engine_unverified`, `retained_ambiguous`, or `retained_read_error` — log it as retained/skipped in the closure artifact's `Source artifact cleanup` section and leave it untouched in `.backlogit/queue/`.

Acceptance criteria:
1. `.github/skills/shipment-reconcile/SKILL.md` Step 0(c), Cascade Close Sub-Procedure step 3 (including the pre/post retained-deliberation preservation check and exclusion of retained deliberations from both `required_ids` and `allowed_ids`), post-cascade source-artifact cleanup, and Quality Criteria agree with `autoharness.gates.shipment_closure.compute_linked_deliberation_disposition` and backlogit 1.12 engine behavior.
2. `.github/agents/_ship.agent.md` Post-Merge Closure Step 7 ("Source artifact cleanup") honors the same INV-12 retention dispositions and live-reference checks so a `retained_shared_reference`, `retained_live_status`, `retained_engine_unverified`, `retained_ambiguous`, or `retained_read_error` deliberation is never archived during post-merge cleanup, and a `retained_description_mention` deliberation is archived only when `status == "queued"` with zero remaining live references in `.backlogit/queue/`.
3. Unconditional `required_ids` membership for the shipment record, qualifying feature members, and non-archived manifest tasks is preserved unchanged, and any newly archived retained deliberation triggers `unexpected-cascade` and `git-revert-on-cascade`.
