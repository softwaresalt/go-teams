internal/transcript: Writer.WriteEvent interpolates Teams-controlled Speaker/Text directly into Markdown, so a crafted display name or caption can inject Markdown/HTML (headings, links, emphasis, code, tables, strikethrough) or break line structure via newlines, and bare URL/email autolinks render clickable. Add a sanitizer that (1) replaces each control char and Unicode line/paragraph separator with a space, (2) backslash-escapes Markdown-structural runes (backtick, *, _, [, ], <, >, #, |, ~), and (3) defuses bare URL and email autolinks with a zero-width space. Speaker and Text are sanitized before formatting. Table-driven tests for injection vectors and that ordinary text stays readable. Scope: writer.go + writer_test.go.
